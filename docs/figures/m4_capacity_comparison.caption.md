**M4 RQ codebook capacity.** Three independent pipeline seeds compare 16 and 32 entries per residual codebook under matched generator capacity. Panels (a–b) show full-test Recall@100 and MAP@20; small connected points are paired seeds and large diamonds show mean ± sample SD. Panel (c) reports RQ-32 minus RQ-16 Recall@100 in percentage points with per-seed 95% paired user-bootstrap intervals (1,000 resamples), conditional on fitted models. All arms use the same 22,225 eligible warm-item test users, full seen-item exclusions and beam 200. Wider beams are validation diagnostics only. Pipeline SD and user intervals measure different uncertainty; users across seeds are not pooled. These test users were previously evaluated in M2/M3.
